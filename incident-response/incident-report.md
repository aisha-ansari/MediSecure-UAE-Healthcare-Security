# Incident Report — Suspected Unauthorized Access to Patient Records

## 1. Incident Details

- Incident ID: MSU-IR-001
- Organization: MediSecure UAE (fictional)
- Incident type: Suspected unauthorized access to sensitive data
- Preliminary severity: High (simulated)
- Status: Tabletop scenario; no real incident occurred

## 2. Scenario

A security alert indicates potentially unauthorized access to a storage resource intended for simulated patient records.

The security team must determine whether the activity was legitimate, identify the affected identity and resource, and assess whether sensitive information may have been accessed.

## 3. Potential Impact

- Unauthorized disclosure of sensitive health information.
- Privacy and regulatory implications.
- Loss of patient and stakeholder trust.
- Potential operational and financial consequences.

These are potential impacts. No actual exposure has been established.

## 4. Investigation Steps

1. Record the alert details and preserve relevant evidence.
2. Identify the affected storage resource and account.
3. Review available audit events, including identity, timestamp, source information, and action performed.
4. Determine whether the activity was authorized.
5. Review identity permissions and storage access settings.
6. Document the evidence available and any remaining uncertainties.
7. Escalate suspected exposure to the appropriate security, privacy, legal, and management stakeholders.

## 5. Containment and Remediation

- Restrict unauthorized access using approved administrative procedures.
- Revoke or disable compromised credentials when justified by the evidence.
- Correct excessive permissions or unintended public exposure.
- Preserve evidence and document response actions.
- Validate corrective controls before closing the incident.

## 6. Recovery and Follow-up

- Confirm that access restrictions work as intended.
- Review relevant logs for additional suspicious activity.
- Assess data integrity and restore affected systems if necessary.
- Document lessons learned and assign remediation owners.
- Evaluate applicable legal or regulatory notification obligations.

## 7. Evidence and Limitations

This is a simulated incident. No live AWS logs, real patient data, or actual unauthorized access were involved.

The scenario does not establish whether data was accessed or exfiltrated. A real investigation would require verified evidence and appropriate authorization.

## 8. Lessons Learned

Effective incident response depends on appropriate logging, restricted permissions, clear escalation paths, evidence preservation, and tested recovery procedures.
